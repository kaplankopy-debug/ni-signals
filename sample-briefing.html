For suppliers of cleaning, security, fit-out, furniture and workplace technology across Northern Ireland.

Here are five real, publicly reported projects and changes. Each includes what happened, who it could matter to and the next question to ask. Potential supplier needs are our interpretation—not confirmed purchasing intentions.

Where to focus first

  • Wall Group: check remaining redevelopment packages.
  • DHL Supply Chain: establish the developer and procurement route.
  • Roe Valley Resort: check later refurbishment phases.

Research snapshot, not a tender list. No organisation has confirmed an open requirement to NI Signals. These stories are already public; this sample tests whether the selection, timing assessment and next steps save you useful research time.

Upcoming redevelopment

01 / Springfield Road, Belfast

Wall Group

Source date: 7 September 2026 · Checked: 8 September 2026

Reported change
The Irish News reports a final redevelopment phase at EUROSPAR Springfield Road, with completion planned for May 2027. Wall Group’s own site also describes the store as being redeveloped.
Potential supplier fit · NI Signals interpretation
Signage, exterior works, security and post-construction cleaning.
Timing assessment · Check remaining packages now
A future completion date leaves a possible window for later packages. The internal refurbishment has already happened; pitching a complete internal fit-out would miss that distinction.
Practical next step
Ask Wall Group who manages the remaining external works and whether any relevant supplier packages are still unassigned.
What remains unverified
No open tender or unappointed supplier package verified. Existing retail and contractor relationships may cover the work.

Planning approval reported

02 / Ballyearl Business Park, Newtownabbey

DHL Supply Chain

Source date: 25 August / 1 September 2026 · Checked: 8 September 2026

Reported change
Reports describe approval for a new warehouse at Ballyearl and a target to be operational around mid-2027. JH Turkington is identified as the developer behind the application.
Potential supplier fit · NI Signals interpretation
Warehouse security, cleaning, office furniture and building-services subcontractors.
Timing assessment · Establish the procurement route
Construction and operational supply are different routes. A local supplier may have a better route through the developer or delivery contractor than through DHL’s general inbox.
Practical next step
Identify the project procurement contact through JH Turkington; ask which packages are open and which are controlled by DHL.
What remains unverified
Council decision document and tender availability not independently retrieved. Reports disagree on job totals, so no jobs figure is used. National frameworks may limit local access.

Phased refurbishment

03 / Limavady

Roe Valley Resort / Galgorm Collection

Source date: 8 June 2026 · Checked: 8 September 2026

Reported change
The resort’s announcement schedules lobby and reception upgrades for Christmas 2026, followed by an indoor/outdoor adult spa in spring 2027.
Potential supplier fit · NI Signals interpretation
Hospitality furniture, signage, specialist finishes and cleaning.
Timing assessment · Check the later phases
This is an older announcement with future phases, not breaking news. Separate the lobby and spa requirements from summer facilities that may already be complete.
Practical next step
Ask the resort to route a supplier enquiry to the team managing the lobby or spa phase, and confirm which specifications and appointments remain open.
What remains unverified
Dates are announced targets, not a verified current works schedule. Supplier selection may already be complete; no invitation to quote has been found.
EvidenceRoe Valley Resort · company announcement, 8 Jun 2026 ↗
Site already staffing up

04 / Moneyreagh, Greater Belfast

SJH Fulfilment

Source date: 4 September 2026 · Checked: 8 September 2026

Reported change
Business Eye reports a new 46,000 sq ft facility and says five employees are already recruited there. Freight Business Journal locates it at Maryland Industrial Estate, Moneyreagh.
Potential supplier fit · NI Signals interpretation
Recurring industrial cleaning, equipment servicing and incremental workplace needs.
Timing assessment · Operational services only
The operational ramp-up is the useful angle. This is not evidence of an untouched building awaiting a full fit-out.
Practical next step
Ask the operations team whether facilities services are handled internally, by existing suppliers or through a review as the site scales.
What remains unverified
No current buying request verified. Initial contracts may be placed already. The company website could not be retrieved during this check; evidence is published reporting.
EvidenceBusiness Eye · 4 Sep 2026 ↗Freight Business Journal · 4 Sep 2026 ↗
Near opening

05 / The Boulevard, Banbridge

Hollywood Bowl

Source date: Announcement body: May 2026; header: Sep 2026 · Checked: 8 September 2026

Reported change
Hollywood Bowl announces an autumn 2026 opening at The Boulevard. Its competition terms also reference a September press night ahead of public opening.
Potential supplier fit · NI Signals interpretation
Opening support or ongoing facilities services, only if a local procurement gap exists.
Timing assessment · Lower priority / verify first
An imminent opening is usually late for major fit-out. Treat this as an operational-services check, not an early construction lead.
Practical next step
Confirm whether the centre has opened, then ask whether facilities suppliers are appointed locally or through group procurement.
What remains unverified
Exact opening day and unfilled needs are unverified. The announcement has inconsistent date labels. Group contracts may cover all relevant services.
