NI SIGNALS / PRIVACY

Your information.
Your choice.

Last updated: 8 September 2026

This notice covers the NI Signals landing site, requests to join our free email briefing, and enquiries about early access. Contact the person operating NI Signals at kap1an_t3@hotmail.com for any privacy question or request.

What we collect

If you send us a signup request, we receive your email address, your request to receive the briefing and launch updates, and any other information you include. For enquiries, we receive your message and contact details. Please only share information needed for your request.

How signup works

The signup form prepares a message in your own email app. Typing into the form does not send your details to NI Signals. You must send the message to request a place on the manually managed list. There is no automatic subscriber database on this website.

Why we use your information

We use your signup request and consent to manage the free briefing and occasional launch updates. We use enquiry details to reply and discuss the service you asked about. An early-access enquiry alone does not subscribe you to marketing emails.

Business outreach and sample feedback

We may use relevant business contact details published on a company’s own website to ask whether our research would be useful. Where those details identify a person, we assess our legitimate interest in introducing a relevant business service against their privacy interests. We record the source, reason for contact, replies and opt-outs privately. We do not add outreach recipients to the free briefing list without their consent.

We check whether unsolicited email is permitted for the recipient’s business type before sending. You can reply “no thanks” or contact the address above to stop further outreach. Unresponsive outreach records are reviewed for deletion after 90 days, apart from a minimal suppression record needed to honour an opt-out. Feedback is used to improve the service and is not published with your name or business without permission.

Storage and service providers

Messages are handled through the NI Signals contact mailbox hosted by Microsoft. Your own email provider also handles messages you send. We do not sell subscriber information or publish it in this website’s GitHub repository.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses and request logs, to operate and secure its hosting service. See GitHub’s privacy statement and Microsoft’s privacy statement for their practices, including international processing.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not add analytics, advertising cookies, tracking pixels or browser storage. It uses a small script to prepare signup emails. Hosting providers may process technical request data as described above.

Retention and your choices

We retain signup details while you want to receive the briefing. To stop emails, reply “unsubscribe” or email the address above. We will remove you from the active list and retain only a minimal record where needed to honour your opt-out. Enquiries are kept only for as long as needed to handle the conversation and any resulting service relationship.

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, withdraw consent, or raise concerns about its use. Other data-protection rights may apply depending on the circumstances. You can also contact the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page before introducing a new signup platform or materially changing how NI Signals handles subscriber information.
